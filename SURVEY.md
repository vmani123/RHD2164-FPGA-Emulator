# SURVEY — lossless multichannel biosignal compression candidates

Cost-filtered, ranked survey of **lossless** compression methods for the 128-ch
RHD2164 / HD-EMG node (STM32H745 Cortex-M7 and Spartan-7 XC7S25; integer/fixed
only; causal/streaming; must beat per-channel FLAC by exploiting cross-channel
spatial correlation). **This file PROPOSES only** — no measured ratios, no codec
edits (non-negotiable #4). Watch-list methods are never promoted without explicit
human approval. Seeds `compression_spec/candidates.md`.

_Compiled: 2026-07-06 · refreshed 2026-07-10 · refreshed 2026-07-13 (cycle 2026-07-13) · refreshed 2026-07-14 (cycle 4, SURVEY-only) · refreshed 2026-07-16 (cycle 7 survey, SURVEY-only) · refreshed 2026-07-19 (cycle 10 survey, SURVEY-only) · **refreshed 2026-07-28 (cycle 13 survey, SURVEY-only)** · web search (US) + candidates.md + INSIGHTS.md._

- _**This cycle's ranked slate (proposals only — see the recs section for full rationale):**_
  - _**(1, highest) Adaptive/data-dependent 2×2 integer-lifting rotation** (`LMS+Rice+xrot_adaptive`). Knob: replace the retired iklt's fixed θ=45° with a **per-block backward-adaptive quantized integer angle** (θ chosen from the previous **reconstructed** block's neighbour covariance, decoder recomputes → zero side-info), keeping the exact reversible 3-lift shear structure, cascaded over grid neighbour pairs. Why the data says so: iklt's fixed basis got only +8.8% vs the data-dependent single-neighbour's +18.0% on the identical predictor/back-end (experiment 002) — the entire gap is basis mismatch (P3), and adapting the one angle is the minimal multiplierless lever that closes it losslessly. Distinct from the retired **fixed** iklt (data-independent→data-dependent, the axis P3 names decisive) AND from the retired scalar **adaptive-beta** (energy-preserving orthogonal rotation of BOTH channels, cascaded multi-tap, vs an asymmetric rank-1 subtract of one)._
  - _**(2) tANS/rANS residual back-end vs Rice** (the entropy axis, untouched all 4 cycles). Knob: swap the adaptive Golomb-Rice coder for a table-driven tANS (LOCO-ANS style) on the SAME `LMS+Rice+xchan` predictor+front-end. Why: every cycle so far moved only the cross-channel front-end; Rice is optimal only for exactly-geometric residuals and real EMG residual histograms deviate sub-Golomb (P5). Orthogonal to the front-end, so composable with either spatial pick; head-to-head on the same predictor isolates the back-end's marginal bits._
  - _**(3) Adaptive CAR (rank-1 common-mode) front-end**, the cheapest untried spatial axis. Knob: subtract a reversible-integer running array-mean (S-transform lift) per time-slice before LMS, gated per block. Why: removes the **shared common-mode** (drive/power-line/reference drift) that pairwise subtraction structurally cannot cancel — a different slice of the cross-channel mutual information (P1) than neighbour rotations — at near-free O(1)/sample-ch amortized cost. Survey rec #3b, still unmeasured._

  - _**(2) Multi-parent backward-adaptive rank-1 subtract** (`LMS+Rice+xchan_multiparent`). Axis: **spatial topology**.   - _**(3, NOVEL) Cross-channel context-adaptive Rice** (`LMS+Rice+xctx`). Axis: **second-order / conditional entropy** — untouched by any tried codec. Knob: keep the adaptive Golomb-Rice coder, but **select the per-sample Rice parameter k for channel c from a backward context formed by the recently-decoded neighbour residual magnitudes** (a spatial-energy bucket → k table; decoder mirrors it → zero side-info). Why (information-theoretic): every prior spatial lever removes correlated **means** (first-order). HD-EMG activity is bursty and **spatially coherent** — a motor-unit action potential raises residual *variance* across a whole electrode region at once — so the residual is **heteroscedastic and cross-channel variance-correlated even after mean decorrelation**. A single per-block k per channel cannot track that; conditioning k on neighbour energy exploits H(e_c | neighbour energy) < H(e_c), lowering **conditional** coded entropy. **This is NOT the retired entropy-coder swap** (`xchan_tans`, P5): the coder engine stays Rice — only its parameter's *context* is enriched with cross-channel information. P5's "Rice is at the entropy floor" concerns the *unconditional per-block* residual; candidate (3) attacks the across-channel heteroscedasticity a per-block k structurally misses. This is the JPEG-LS/CALIC context-modeling principle (condition the Golomb parameter on a causal context) but with a **spatial cross-channel** context matched to the array's variance coherence — a lever nothing here has tried. Embeddable: a few adds to accumulate neighbour |e| + a context→k lookup, backward-adaptive, RTL-trivial. Risk to measure: if the per-block k already tracks local variance well, the cross-channel context may add little — a clean, cheap test settles it._

  - _**(1, TOP PICK — NOVEL) Delay-compensated spatio-temporal cross-channel predictor** (`LMS4+Rice+xstfir`). Axis: **cross-channel LAG — a spatial degree of freedom no registered codec has ever varied.** Knob: replace the scalar `β·x_p[n]` rank-1 subtract with a **short (2–3 tap) jointly-adapted FIR over the parent's reconstructed history** `{x_p[n], x_p[n−1], x_p[n−2]}` — and, optionally, one tap on an **opposite-side neighbour from the fully-reconstructed previous time slice** `x_q[n−1]` (available for *any* channel, including ones not yet coded at time n). One joint sign-sign LMS over the tap vector, asymmetric (error injected only into the coded residual, parents left clean), zero side-info. **Theory (signal model + Wiener):** HD-sEMG is not a static spatial mixture — motor-unit action potentials **propagate along the muscle fibres at a finite conduction velocity** (~3–5 m/s), so at 8–10 mm inter-electrode distance the *same* MUAP reaches the neighbour with a delay of a fraction of a sample to several samples. The optimal single-parent predictor between two sensors observing a **delayed** common source is a **fractional-delay filter, not a gain**: a zero-lag scalar β can only remove the projection onto ρ(0), leaving the mismatched-delay energy `ρ(τ*)² − ρ(0)²` in the residual **no matter how the parent is selected or how many parents are added**. That is a plausible identity for the ~1% ceiling ten zero-lag variants share, and it is the one term none of them can reach. Direct precedent: **MPEG-4 ALS's own inter-channel prediction is a three-tap filter with an explicit transmitted lag** (Liebchen; ALS "Extended linear prediction tools"), i.e. the standard this project cites for `+xchan` never intended the 1-tap zero-lag reduction our codecs implement. Verdict **embeddable**: +2–3 int16 taps of spatial state/ch on the order-4 base (≈ +6–8 B/ch), multiplierless sign-sign updates, ~12–20 extra ops/sample-ch → expect cost ~0.045–0.05 vs the best's 0.039; comfortably inside the 1875-cyc sEMG budget and plausible at 125 cyc for neural. **Distinct from RETIRED `xchan_multiparent`** (that summed *marginal* betas over two *parents at lag 0* → double-counted; this is one joint solve over *lags of one parent*, and the taps co-adapt so cannot double-count). **Distinct from KEPT `xchan_joint2` and from PR #6/#7's `jointbp2`/`joint2_bpa`** — those add a second **parent** at **lag 0** (P1b showed parent selection and parent count are *substitutes*, both capped by the same zero-lag ρ(0)); this adds a **lag**, an orthogonal degree of freedom that raises the cap itself. **Distinct from RETIRED `iklt_adaptive`** (energy-preserving rotation corrupting both channels; this is asymmetric residual-only injection, P3-refinement robustness). **Distinct from RETIRED `xres`** (that moved the subtract into the innovation domain at lag 0; this stays in the raw domain — the better-conditioned regressor P1c identified — and moves the *lag*). **Not** a deeper temporal predictor (P2): the extra taps read a **different channel's** past, which carries the delayed copy, not the coding channel's own already-whitened past._
  - _**(2, NOVEL topology class) Maximum-weight spanning-forest channel topology with permuted coding order** (`LMS4+Rice+xtree`). Axis: **global spatial topology + coding order — the parent set and the channel ordering, both previously frozen.** Knob: per block, build a **maximum-weight directed spanning forest** over a bounded candidate edge set of the channel graph (edge weight = backward-measured |normalized cross-correlation| from the previous **reconstructed** block), then code channels in the forest's topological order, each predicted from its tree parent by the already-shipped adaptive rank-1 subtract. Decoder rebuilds the identical forest from the same reconstructed block → **zero side-info**, look-ahead 0. **Theory (Chow–Liu / Tate):** under a first-order (tree-structured) dependency model, the sum of conditional entropies `Σ_c H(x_c | x_parent(c))` is **minimized exactly by the maximum-weight spanning tree** whose edge weights are the pairwise mutual informations (Chow & Liu 1968) — and Tate (IEEE Trans. Computers 1997) proves the directly analogous statement for lossless **inter-band** prediction: the optimal coding order is a **maximum-weight directed spanning forest**, with substantial reported gains over the natural (raster) order (paper-reported, unverified here). Our shipped best-partner is a *raster-constrained, 4-neighbourhood, greedy* forest — provably **≤** the Chow–Liu optimum, and the gap is exactly what this measures. **Why it can move the number where nothing else has:** the constraint bites hardest precisely where the harness sees no gain. **CapgMyo** is a **differential** 8×16 array (neighbour |corr| ≈ 0.29, +1.3% gain — every spatial lever's honest negative control): adjacent differential channels share an electrode with opposite sign, so the physically best-correlated partner may sit at distance 2, along the other grid axis, or be *anti*-correlated — a global forest finds it, a 4-neighbourhood raster scan structurally cannot. Likewise on the 128-/320-ch **Hyser/CEMHSEY** multi-grid mountings, a raster `up`/`left` parent can be on a different grid or muscle entirely, contributing near-zero MI; the forest will never select such an edge. Verdict **borderline → embeddable, cost is the real gate**: a full 128×128 per-block correlation matrix is ~64 MAC/sample-ch (blows the 125-cyc neural budget, eats ~3% of the sEMG one) and must be avoided — the embeddable form uses (a) a **bounded candidate edge set** (~8–16 edges/ch: the 8-neighbourhood plus the previous block's surviving top-M), (b) **sign-sign correlation counters** (XOR + increment, multiplierless) and (c) **time-subsampled accumulation** (every 8th sample), giving ~1 op/sample-ch, with one Prim/Kruskal pass over ≤2048 edges per 512-sample block (amortized negligible). Gate hard on that construction. **Distinct from DONE `LMS4+Rice+xchan_bestpartner` and `..._bestpartner_adaptive`** — those select **one parent per channel from the fixed 4-neighbourhood under a fixed raster coding order**; this makes the **candidate set global and the coding order itself a free variable**, optimized by a global criterion (total forest weight) rather than greedily per channel. It is the "widen the candidate set beyond the 4-neighbourhood / channel-clustering reference selection" follow-up this SURVEY has carried as untried since cycle 2. **Not** a multi-tap transform (P3): every edge is still the shipped, verified single-parent rank-1 adaptive subtract — only *which* edges exist changes._
  - _**(3, distinct temporal scale) Gated long-term (MUAP-firing-period) prediction** (`LMS4+Rice+ltp`). Axis: **long-range temporal structure — a lag scale ~50× beyond anything tried.** Knob: after the order-4 short-term LMS, subtract a **single backward-adaptive tap at a long lag T** from the residual: `e'[n] = e[n] − round(g·e[n−T])`, with T picked per block per channel as the argmax of the previous **reconstructed** residual's autocorrelation over a bounded range (≈64–200 samples at 2 kS/s) and **gated** — applied only when that normalized autocorrelation clears a threshold, so it cannot hurt (the ACAR gate pattern, already verified to fire/not-fire cleanly). Decoder mirrors both T and the gate → zero side-info. **Theory:** motor units fire as **quasi-periodic trains** at 8–30 pps with low ISI variability, so each channel's signal contains a component correlated with itself at lag T ≈ 67–250 samples. An order-4 filter spans 2 ms and is **structurally blind** to that lag; a whiteness test over a short window will call the residual white while this long-lag correlation remains. This is exactly the redundancy **MPEG-4 ALS added a dedicated Long-Term Prediction stage for** (5 weighted long-lag residues, lags "hundreds of samples"), on the explicit grounds that "distant sample correlations are difficult to remove with the standard forward-adaptive predictor, since very high orders would be required" — the same argument, transplanted from pitch harmonics to MU firing periodicity. **Distinct from P2's dead lever** (that is *order* of the short-term filter, taps 5–8 at lags 5–8 — this is one tap at lag ~100, not reachable by raising the order without an absurd tap count and the noise-fitting P2 warns about). **Distinct from the freshly-retired regime-switched predictor banks** (PR #6 `LMS4rs`, PR #7 `LMS4x2`, P4b): those **switch coefficients by regime** on the same short support and so re-fit noise; this **extends the support** to a lag the short filter cannot see, adding one tap, not a bank. Verdict **borderline, sEMG-only**: needs a per-channel residual ring buffer of T_max samples — at T_max = 200, 128 ch × int16 ≈ 51 kB (fine in the STM32H745's SRAM; ~1/4 of the XC7S25's ~202 kB BRAM after the playback loop's ~16/45 BRAM36k — leaves headroom but must be budgeted), plus one lag search per block per channel (bounded, subsample-able). **Flag: fails the 30 kS/s neural profile** — the lag range scales ×15 (memory ×15) and neural spike trains are far less periodic, so the gate should be compiled out for neural. Honest risk to measure: at high contraction force HD-sEMG is **interference EMG** (tens of MUs superimposed per channel), which smears the composite autocorrelation toward white — the gate may simply never fire on the high-force recordings, in which case the codec is bit-identical to the base and the lever is cheaply settled either way. Lowest confidence of the three; ranked third for that reason._

## Recommendations — next codec to implement THIS cycle (embeddable, by expected gain/cost)

### CURRENT — cycle 13 (2026-07-28)

The ledger is now unusually informative: **ten registered spatial variants** (one parent vs two;
fixed vs selected vs jointly-solved; global-CAR vs local pairwise; cascaded vs single-stage;
raw-domain vs innovation-domain) all land **within ~1% of each other**. Read as ten independent
failures that is discouraging; read correctly it is **one shared structural constraint**. Every
one of them holds two assumptions nobody has ever varied: the cross-channel predictor reads the
parent **only at time index n** (a scalar gain is the degenerate zero-lag 1-tap case of a
cross-channel filter), and the parent must be a **raster-order grid neighbour** with the channel
coding order frozen. This slate varies exactly those two, plus one untouched temporal *scale*.
None is a parameter variant of any registered or retired codec, and none is on the four
mechanisms the two unmerged sibling PRs just spent.

1. **★★ TOP PICK — Delay-compensated spatio-temporal cross-channel predictor
   (`LMS4+Rice+xstfir`, table G).** Highest payoff **and** lowest mechanism risk: it reuses the
   joint sign-sign LMS already proven to work and to be robust (`xchan_joint2`, P1b), and changes
   only *what the taps read*. HD-sEMG is not a static spatial mixture — MUAPs **propagate** along
   the muscle fibres at ~3–5 m/s, so at 8–10 mm IED the same waveform arrives at the neighbour
   **delayed**. Wiener theory is unambiguous here: the optimal predictor from a **delayed** common
   source is a **fractional-delay filter, not a gain**, so a zero-lag scalar β leaves
   `ρ(τ*)² − ρ(0)²` of the shared energy in the residual **no matter which parent is chosen or how
   many parents are added** — which is a concrete, falsifiable identity for the ~1% ceiling the
   whole zero-lag family shares. The precedent is in the very standard this project cites for
   `+xchan`: **MPEG-4 ALS's inter-channel prediction is a three-tap filter with an explicit lag**;
   our codecs implement only its 1-tap zero-lag reduction. Verdict **embeddable** (+2–3 int16
   taps/ch, multiplierless, est. cost ~0.045–0.05 vs the best's 0.039). Distinctions from the
   retired/kept neighbours are stated in table G — the load-bearing one: **`joint2` and PR#6/#7's
   fusions add a second *parent* at lag 0, and P1b showed selection and count are *substitutes*
   because both are capped by ρ(0); adding a *lag* is the orthogonal degree of freedom that raises
   the cap itself.**
2. **★★ Maximum-weight spanning-forest channel topology with permuted coding order
   (`LMS4+Rice+xtree`, table H).** The second frozen assumption: the parent set and the coding
   order. Chow–Liu (1968) says the tree-structured predictor minimizing `Σ_c H(x_c|x_parent(c))`
   is exactly the **maximum-weight spanning tree** on the pairwise-MI graph; Tate (IEEE Trans.
   Computers 1997) proves the directly analogous result for lossless **inter-band** prediction —
   the optimal coding order is a **maximum-weight directed spanning forest**, with substantial
   reported gains over the natural raster order (paper-reported, unverified here). The shipped
   best-partner is a *raster-constrained, 4-neighbourhood, greedy* forest and is therefore
   **provably ≤** that optimum; the gap is what this measures. It should bite hardest exactly
   where the harness has always seen nothing: **CapgMyo**'s differential 8×16 array (adjacent
   differential channels share an electrode with opposite sign, so the true best partner may be at
   distance 2, on the other axis, or anti-correlated — invisible to a 4-neighbourhood raster scan)
   and the multi-grid **Hyser/CEMHSEY** mountings (a raster `up` parent can sit on a different
   grid or muscle). Backward-adaptive from the previous reconstructed block → zero side-info.
   Verdict **borderline → embeddable, and cost is the real gate**: the naive per-block 128×128
   correlation matrix (~64 MAC/sample-ch) is disqualifying — the implementation **must** use a
   bounded candidate edge set, sign-sign correlation counters and time-subsampled accumulation
   (~1 op/sample-ch). Ranked second only because of that cost risk, not the theory.
3. **Gated long-term (MUAP-firing-period) prediction (`LMS4+Rice+ltp`, table I).** A third,
   genuinely different axis: **temporal scale**. Every temporal lever tried has lived at lags 1–8;
   motor units fire as quasi-periodic trains at 8–30 pps, putting real self-correlation at lag
   ≈67–250 samples, where an order-4 (2 ms) filter is structurally blind — a short-window
   whiteness test will call that residual white while the long-lag term survives. This is the exact
   redundancy **MPEG-4 ALS added a dedicated LTP stage for**, and its stated rationale ("distant
   sample correlations are difficult to remove with the standard forward-adaptive predictor, since
   very high orders would be required") transplants cleanly from pitch harmonics to MU firing
   periodicity. One backward-selected tap, gated on the measured autocorrelation so it cannot hurt.
   Explicitly **not** P2's dead order lever (that is taps at lags 5–8) and **not** the freshly
   retired regime-switched banks (those switch *coefficients* on the same short support and re-fit
   noise, P4b; this *extends the support*). Verdict **borderline, sEMG-only** — ~51 kB of residual
   ring buffer and a lag search per block, and it **fails the 30 kS/s neural profile** (memory ×15,
   spike trains far less periodic). Ranked third on confidence: at high contraction force
   interference EMG (tens of superimposed MUs per channel) smears the composite autocorrelation
   toward white and the gate may simply never fire — in which case it is bit-identical to the base
   and the lever is cheaply and permanently settled.

Order reflects expected value per unit embedded cost on the real grids: a cheap, low-mechanism-risk
change that plausibly identifies and lifts the ceiling every zero-lag spatial codec shares (#1) >
a theoretically-optimal topology whose only real risk is the cost of building it on-node (#2) > a
distinct temporal scale with a clean cannot-hurt gate but honest signal-model risk (#3). The three
are distinct in mechanism — **cross-channel lag** vs **global topology/coding order** vs
**long-range temporal support** — not parameter variants of one another; each names the prior
retired/kept attempt nearest to it and why it differs.

### HISTORICAL — cycle 7 slate (superseded, kept for the record)

The prior slate (cycle 7) settled the temporal axis: **order-4 best-partner
(`LMS4+Rice+xchan_bestpartner`) was PROMOTED as the new leaderboard best** and is now
the incumbent to beat; its two siblings (summed multi-parent, cross-channel context Rice)
were both RETIRED. The dead ledger now holds 7 codecs — every entropy-back-end swap (P5),
every context-model of the Rice parameter (P5-ext), every multi-tap inter-channel rotation
fixed OR adaptive (P3), the summed multi-parent subtract (P1-ref), and the single-parent
scalar β. With every single-parent rank-1 variation and every dead axis exhausted, the
three live levers left are: **combine** the two orthogonal spatial slices, do the second
parent **jointly** (the only escape P3 leaves open), and **close the port caveat** on the
shipped best. This cycle's slate takes one of each — distinct in mechanism, not parameter
variants.

1. **★ TOP PICK — Two-stage scale-matched front-end: global ACAR then local order-4
   best-partner (`acar+lms4bp`, table D).** The highest payoff/cost live lever and the
   lowest mechanism risk: both stages are already implemented and verified bit-exact —
   this only *composes* them. Run the reversible-integer CAR lift first (it removes the
   rank-1 **global** DC-across-array common mode; +14.4% real on tight OTB, non-dominated),
   then the promoted order-4 best-partner subtract on the CAR **residual** (it removes
   **local pairwise** MI). CAR and best-partner capture *different, non-interchangeable*
   slices of P1's cross-channel MI, and array size selects which dominates (CAR on tight
   arrays, pairwise on large) — cascading them captures **both** where both exist. Unlike
   the retired summed multi-parent (which double-counted two correlated *local* parents),
   the two stages are **orthogonal by construction** (a global mean is uncorrelated with the
   local pairwise residual it leaves), so they cannot double-count. Verdict **embeddable**
   (CAR is amortized O(1)/sample-ch on top of lms4bp's 0.039). Distinct axis (cascaded
   spatial scales) from #2/#3. INSIGHTS open-frontier #1. Risk to measure: on large arrays
   CAR may not clear its per-block gate — settle whether the slices are additive or, after
   best-partner already took the local slice, redundant.
2. **NOVEL — Joint asymmetric 2-parent adaptive sign-LMS predictor
   (`LMS+Rice+xchan_joint2`, table E).** The concrete, de-risked realization of the ONE
   spatial escape P3 left open: a *joint* (not summed, not rotational) second-parent solve.
   Predict channel c from BOTH causal grid parents (up + left) with a **single joint
   backward-adaptive sign-sign LMS** whose two spatial taps co-adapt against the shared
   post-subtraction residual, injecting error only into the coded residual and leaving both
   parents clean. Zero side-info, multiplierless. This explicitly overcomes both prior
   failures. **vs RETIRED `xchan_multiparent`:** that summed two *marginal* betas (each
   estimated as if its parent were the sole regressor) and so double-counted the correlated
   parents' shared mode → over-subtracted; a joint gradient adapts the taps together against
   the *actual* residual after all current taps, the marginal→multiple regression fix the
   sum lacked. **vs RETIRED `iklt_adaptive`:** that was an energy-preserving Givens rotation
   that mixes and corrupts BOTH channels under a stale/noisy angle; the joint predictor is
   **asymmetric** (rank-1 residual-only injection), so estimation noise never touches the
   parents — the robustness property (P3-refinement) that a rotation structurally lacks.
   Grounded in the MPEG-4 ALS RLS-LMS multichannel / multivariate-RLS direction
   (candidates.md; arXiv 1605.04418). Verdict **borderline → embeddable**: +1 spatial tap's
   state/ops on the order-4 base; gate hard on the neural 125-cyc budget. Distinct axis
   (joint 2-parent predictor) from #1/#3. INSIGHTS open-frontier #3.
3. **Backward-adaptive per-block best-partner RE-SELECTION (`lms4bp_adaptive`, table F).**
   An **embeddability / port-caveat closure**, not a ratio play — but a genuinely distinct
   axis (parameter estimation / side-info elimination) and high-value: it makes the shipped
   leaderboard best fully on-node. The promoted `LMS4+Rice+xchan_bestpartner` derives its
   partner id + β **offline over the whole recording** — its last non-embeddable caveat (P4).
   Re-select partner id + integer β per block from the previous **reconstructed** block's
   4-neighbourhood cross-correlation; the decoder mirrors the identical selection, so it costs
   **zero side-info** and is streaming-legal, and it *drops* the 2×int16/ch partner+β header.
   Measure whether per-block re-selection holds the offline ratio (non-stationarity risk: a
   stale partner choice across a burst boundary). **Not** a re-run of RETIRED `xchan_adaptive`
   (that was a single *fixed-grid-parent* scalar β with no partner selection at all); this
   re-selects the *partner identity* per block — the exact port closure P4/INSIGHTS
   open-frontier #2 endorse. Verdict **embeddable** (bounded per-block cross-corr, amortized
   cheap; net removes side-info).

Order reflects expected value per unit embedded cost for THIS device on the real grids:
the lowest-risk composition that could raise the ratio on both tight and large arrays (#1)
> the only theoretically-valid second-parent ratio lever left, de-risked into an asymmetric
joint predictor (#2) > a distinct-axis embeddability lever that closes the shipped best's
port caveat at zero side-info (#3). All three are genuinely distinct in mechanism (cascaded
spatial scales vs joint 2-parent predictor vs backward-adaptive parameter estimation), not
parameter variants of each other, and none re-proposes a retired mechanism — the two that
touch retired territory (#2, #3) name the prior attempt and why they differ.
