# SURVEY — lossless multichannel biosignal compression candidates

Cost-filtered, ranked survey of **lossless** compression methods for the 128-ch
RHD2164 / HD-EMG node (STM32H745 Cortex-M7 and Spartan-7 XC7S25; integer/fixed
only; causal/streaming; must beat per-channel FLAC by exploiting cross-channel
spatial correlation). **This file PROPOSES only** — no measured ratios, no codec
edits (non-negotiable #4). Watch-list methods are never promoted without explicit
human approval. Seeds `compression_spec/candidates.md`.

Embeddable: a few adds to accumulate neighbour |e| + a context→k lookup, backward-adaptive, RTL-trivial. Risk to measure: if the per-block k already tracks local variance well, the cross-channel context may add little — a clean, cheap test settles it._

## Recommendations — next codec to implement THIS cycle (cycle 13, 2026-07-25)

Cycle 10 spent all three of its frontier levers and **none beat** `LMS4+Rice+xchan_bestpartner`:
the two-stage cascade helped only on the tight OTB array, the joint 2-parent solve *worked* but
only tied, and the backward-adaptive re-selection closed the port caveat at ~zero ratio cost.
Three distinct attacks on the single-parent rank-1 ceiling all landed at ≤ the best, and INSIGHTS
now reads the spatial levers as clustered within ~1% of a **shared ceiling**. That is the signal
this slate answers: two of the three picks deliberately leave the "which/how many raw neighbours"
question and change *where* the decorrelation happens (#1, residual domain) or *what the predictor
is* (#3, regime switching); #2 is the one remaining stack-not-trade move inside the proven family.

1. **★ TOP PICK — Residual-domain (innovation) inter-channel prediction with a matched coding
   objective (`LMS4+Rice+xres`, table G).** The highest payoff/cost lever and the one structural
   gap this project has never tested. Every cross-channel front-end here — `ec.cross_forward`,
   `_bp_select`, `xj2` — subtracts `β·x_parent` from the **raw** channel *before* `lms_forward`,
   and derives β as the raw least-squares gain `⟨x_c,x_p⟩/⟨x_p,x_p⟩`, scored on raw-difference
   bits. But the bits actually paid are Rice bits on the **residual**, ≈ `log2 E|ê_c|`, so the
   coding-optimal gain is `argmin_β Var(e_c − β e_p)`. Raw-then-temporal equals residual-domain
   **only** when both channels use identical predictors; each channel's LMS taps adapt
   independently, so the incumbent is optimizing the wrong functional — spending its single
   spatial degree of freedom cancelling shared content the temporal predictor would have removed
   for free, while leaving shared **innovation** (the temporally-unpredictable common
   drive/reference/common-mode noise a per-channel predictor structurally cannot reach, P1)
   partly uncoded. Fix: code `ê_c = e_c − (β·e_p >> shift)` against the parent's already-computed
   residual, β backward-adaptive (sign-sign) on the residual pair, partner chosen by **residual**
   cross-correlation — precisely the MPEG-4 ALS multichannel tool ("adaptively weighted
   subtraction of the residual signals … produced by independent linear prediction", up to
   **14.6%** ratio improvement, paper-reported, unverified here) and Choi et al.'s residual-DF
   gate. Verdict **embeddable and cheaper than the raw front-end**: `e_p` is already in registers
   when channel c is coded, so no extra channel buffer; one multiply-shift + add + sign-LMS update
   per sample-ch; zero side-info; look-ahead 0. **Explicitly not RETIRED `xctx`** — that left the
   coded value untouched and only conditioned the Rice **parameter** on neighbour *energy* (a
   variance/second-order lever that died because H(e_c | neighbour energy) ≈ H(e_c) after LMS);
   this **subtracts a weighted neighbour residual**, removing first-order correlation between
   innovations. Risk to measure: if the per-channel LMS taps converge to near-identical values the
   two domains nearly coincide and the gain collapses — a cheap, decisive experiment either way.
2. **Best-partner SELECTION fused with the joint 2-parent solve (`LMS4+Rice+xchan_joint2_bpa`,
   table H).** INSIGHTS open-frontier #1 and the last stack-not-trade move inside the proven
   rank-1 family: jointly solve (co-adaptive sign-sign LMS, exactly `xchan_joint2`) the best
   **pair** of causal neighbours, the pair re-selected per block from the previous *reconstructed*
   block (exactly `bestpartner_adaptive` → zero side-info), instead of a fixed up+left pair.
   `joint2` produced the highest Hyser cross-channel gain of any registered codec (+12.26%) — the
   second parent genuinely carries MI on large arrays — but lost tight OTB because its pair was
   *fixed* where *selection* dominates; best-partner wins tight arrays by selection but is
   single-parent. P1b proved count and selection are **substitutes**; fusing them is the only
   untried way to stack rather than trade. Both ingredients are implemented and unanimously
   verified, so this is a composition, not a new primitive. Verdict **borderline → embeddable**:
   cap the candidate set to the 4-neighbourhood's 6 pairs and gate hard on the 125-cyc neural
   budget. Ranked below #1 because P1b's substitution may simply bind again — a fourth tie at the
   same spatial ceiling — whereas #1 changes the ceiling's definition.
3. **NOVEL — Regime-switched temporal predictor bank (`LMS4x2+Rice+xchan_bestpartner` / `swlms`,
   table I).** INSIGHTS open-frontier #2, and the only pick on a non-spatial axis. Keep order-4
   sign-sign LMS and the shipped best-partner front-end, but hold **K=2** tap-sets per channel and
   select the active one from a causally-derived activity state (quantized bit-length of a leaky
   |e| integrator with hysteresis; the decoder mirrors it → zero side-info). Signal-model
   rationale: interference HD-sEMG is **amplitude-modulated** — colored EMG gated by a slow
   activation envelope over an instrumentation-noise baseline — and the MMSE tap vector depends on
   the **normalized** autocorrelation, which differs between the two regimes (near-white rest ⇒
   optimal taps ≈ 0; colored burst ⇒ taps ≠ 0). One adaptive filter converges to a power-weighted
   compromise dominated by the active regime, so it over-predicts at rest and lags by its
   convergence time at every burst onset. A state-indexed bank removes both, cutting residual
   variance in *both* regimes; since Rice length ≈ `log2 E|e|`, that is directly fewer bits. This
   attacks the entropy floor **upstream in the predictor** — P5/P5-ext closed only the
   **downstream** coder engine and its parameter's context, and P2 caps predictor *order*, not the
   number of *regimes*. Verdict **embeddable**: ops/sample-ch unchanged (one tap-set active),
   +leaky integrator +compare, state ×K (8 int32/ch at K=2). Risk: context fragmentation — each
   tap-set adapts on ~1/K of the samples, the exact mechanism that sank `xctx`'s 12 buckets — so
   keep K=2 with hysteresis and order 4, and only widen K if K=2 pays.

Order reflects expected value per unit embedded cost on the real grids: a structural
objective/domain fix that is *cheaper* than the incumbent front-end and untested here (#1) > the
last stack-not-trade composition inside the proven spatial family (#2) > a genuinely different
(temporal) axis where the bottleneck has plausibly moved but whose failure mode is known (#3).
All three are distinct in **mechanism** — the domain in which the cross-channel subtract is
applied, the topology/count of raw spatial parents, and the temporal predictor's regime structure
— not parameter variants of each other. None re-proposes a retired mechanism; #1 and #3 name the
retired attempt they neighbour (`xctx`) and state precisely how they differ. A fourth idea,
**Chow–Liu maximum-MI spanning-tree pairing** (table J), is recorded but deferred: it refines the
same selection lever already at the shared spatial ceiling.

## Recommendations — prior cycle (cycle 10, kept for the record)

The prior slate (cycle 7) settled the temporal axis: **order-4 best-partner
(`LMS4+Rice+xchan_bestpartner`) was PROMOTED as the new leaderboard best** and is now
the incumbent to beat; its two siblings (summed multi-parent, cross-channel context Rice)
were both RETIRED. The dead ledger now holds 7 codecs — every entropy-back-end swap (P5),
every context-model of the Rice parameter (P5-ext), every multi-tap inter-channel rotation
fixed OR adaptive (P3), the summed multi-parent subtract (P1-ref), and the single-parent
scalar β. With every single-parent rank-1 variation and every dead axis exhausted, the
three live levers left are: **combine** the two orthogonal spatial slices, do the second
parent **jointly** (the only escape P3 leaves open), and **close the port caveat** on the
shipped best. This cycle's slate takes one of each — distinct in mechanism, not parameter
variants.

1. **★ TOP PICK — Two-stage scale-matched front-end: global ACAR then local order-4
   best-partner (`acar+lms4bp`, table D).** The highest payoff/cost live lever and the
   lowest mechanism risk: both stages are already implemented and verified bit-exact —
   this only *composes* them. Run the reversible-integer CAR lift first (it removes the
   rank-1 **global** DC-across-array common mode; +14.4% real on tight OTB, non-dominated),
   then the promoted order-4 best-partner subtract on the CAR **residual** (it removes
   **local pairwise** MI). CAR and best-partner capture *different, non-interchangeable*
   slices of P1's cross-channel MI, and array size selects which dominates (CAR on tight
   arrays, pairwise on large) — cascading them captures **both** where both exist. Unlike
   the retired summed multi-parent (which double-counted two correlated *local* parents),
   the two stages are **orthogonal by construction** (a global mean is uncorrelated with the
   local pairwise residual it leaves), so they cannot double-count. Verdict **embeddable**
   (CAR is amortized O(1)/sample-ch on top of lms4bp's 0.039). Distinct axis (cascaded
   spatial scales) from #2/#3. INSIGHTS open-frontier #1. Risk to measure: on large arrays
   CAR may not clear its per-block gate — settle whether the slices are additive or, after
   best-partner already took the local slice, redundant.
2. **NOVEL — Joint asymmetric 2-parent adaptive sign-LMS predictor
   (`LMS+Rice+xchan_joint2`, table E).** The concrete, de-risked realization of the ONE
   spatial escape P3 left open: a *joint* (not summed, not rotational) second-parent solve.
   Predict channel c from BOTH causal grid parents (up + left) with a **single joint
   backward-adaptive sign-sign LMS** whose two spatial taps co-adapt against the shared
   post-subtraction residual, injecting error only into the coded residual and leaving both
   parents clean. Zero side-info, multiplierless. This explicitly overcomes both prior
   failures. **vs RETIRED `xchan_multiparent`:** that summed two *marginal* betas (each
   estimated as if its parent were the sole regressor) and so double-counted the correlated
   parents' shared mode → over-subtracted; a joint gradient adapts the taps together against
   the *actual* residual after all current taps, the marginal→multiple regression fix the
   sum lacked. **vs RETIRED `iklt_adaptive`:** that was an energy-preserving Givens rotation
   that mixes and corrupts BOTH channels under a stale/noisy angle; the joint predictor is
   **asymmetric** (rank-1 residual-only injection), so estimation noise never touches the
   parents — the robustness property (P3-refinement) that a rotation structurally lacks.
   Grounded in the MPEG-4 ALS RLS-LMS multichannel / multivariate-RLS direction
   (candidates.md; arXiv 1605.04418). Verdict **borderline → embeddable**: +1 spatial tap's
   state/ops on the order-4 base; gate hard on the neural 125-cyc budget. Distinct axis
   (joint 2-parent predictor) from #1/#3. INSIGHTS open-frontier #3.
3. **Backward-adaptive per-block best-partner RE-SELECTION (`lms4bp_adaptive`, table F).**
   An **embeddability / port-caveat closure**, not a ratio play — but a genuinely distinct
   axis (parameter estimation / side-info elimination) and high-value: it makes the shipped
   leaderboard best fully on-node. The promoted `LMS4+Rice+xchan_bestpartner` derives its
   partner id + β **offline over the whole recording** — its last non-embeddable caveat (P4).
   Re-select partner id + integer β per block from the previous **reconstructed** block's
   4-neighbourhood cross-correlation; the decoder mirrors the identical selection, so it costs
   **zero side-info** and is streaming-legal, and it *drops* the 2×int16/ch partner+β header.
   Measure whether per-block re-selection holds the offline ratio (non-stationarity risk: a
   stale partner choice across a burst boundary). **Not** a re-run of RETIRED `xchan_adaptive`
   (that was a single *fixed-grid-parent* scalar β with no partner selection at all); this
   re-selects the *partner identity* per block — the exact port closure P4/INSIGHTS
   open-frontier #2 endorse. Verdict **embeddable** (bounded per-block cross-corr, amortized
   cheap; net removes side-info).

Order reflects expected value per unit embedded cost for THIS device on the real grids:
the lowest-risk composition that could raise the ratio on both tight and large arrays (#1)
> the only theoretically-valid second-parent ratio lever left, de-risked into an asymmetric
joint predictor (#2) > a distinct-axis embeddability lever that closes the shipped best's
port caveat at zero side-info (#3). All three are genuinely distinct in mechanism (cascaded
spatial scales vs joint 2-parent predictor vs backward-adaptive parameter estimation), not
parameter variants of each other, and none re-proposes a retired mechanism — the two that
touch retired territory (#2, #3) name the prior attempt and why they differ.
