# SURVEY — lossless multichannel biosignal compression candidates

Cost-filtered, ranked survey of **lossless** compression methods for the 128-ch
RHD2164 / HD-EMG node (STM32H745 Cortex-M7 and Spartan-7 XC7S25; integer/fixed
only; causal/streaming; must beat per-channel FLAC by exploiting cross-channel
spatial correlation). **This file PROPOSES only** — no measured ratios, no codec
edits (non-negotiable #4). Watch-list methods are never promoted without explicit
human approval. Seeds `compression_spec/candidates.md`.

Knob: replace the retired iklt's fixed θ=45° with a **per-block backward-adaptive quantized integer angle** (θ chosen from the previous **reconstructed** block's neighbour covariance, decoder recomputes → zero side-info), keeping the exact reversible 3-lift shear structure, cascaded over grid neighbour pairs.   - _**(2) tANS/rANS residual back-end vs Rice** (the entropy axis, untouched all 4 cycles). Knob: swap the adaptive Golomb-Rice coder for a table-driven tANS (LOCO-ANS style) on the SAME `LMS+Rice+xchan` predictor+front-end. Why: every cycle so far moved only the cross-channel front-end; Rice is optimal only for exactly-geometric residuals and real EMG residual histograms deviate sub-Golomb (P5). Survey rec #3b, still unmeasured._

  - _**(3, NOVEL) Cross-channel context-adaptive Rice** (`LMS+Rice+xctx`). Axis: **second-order / conditional entropy** — untouched by any tried codec. Knob: keep the adaptive Golomb-Rice coder, but **select the per-sample Rice parameter k for channel c from a backward context formed by the recently-decoded neighbour residual magnitudes** (a spatial-energy bucket → k table; decoder mirrors it → zero side-info). Why (information-theoretic): every prior spatial lever removes correlated **means** (first-order). HD-EMG activity is bursty and **spatially coherent** — a motor-unit action potential raises residual *variance* across a whole electrode region at once — so the residual is **heteroscedastic and cross-channel variance-correlated even after mean decorrelation**. A single per-block k per channel cannot track that; conditioning k on neighbour energy exploits H(e_c | neighbour energy) < H(e_c), lowering **conditional** coded entropy. **This is NOT the retired entropy-coder swap** (`xchan_tans`, P5): the coder engine stays Rice — only its parameter's *context* is enriched with cross-channel information. P5's "Rice is at the entropy floor" concerns the *unconditional per-block* residual; candidate (3) attacks the across-channel heteroscedasticity a per-block k structurally misses. This is the JPEG-LS/CALIC context-modeling principle (condition the Golomb parameter on a causal context) but with a **spatial cross-channel** context matched to the array's variance coherence — a lever nothing here has tried. Embeddable: a few adds to accumulate neighbour |e| + a context→k lookup, backward-adaptive, RTL-trivial. Risk to measure: if the per-block k already tracks local variance well, the cross-channel context may add little — a clean, cheap test settles it._

Note **P6 constrains placement**: such a stage must not sit ahead of the spatial subtract._

## Verdict key
- **embeddable** — integer, causal, bounded state/look-ahead, fits the sEMG budget
  (≥1831 cyc/sample-ch) and plausibly the 30 kHz neural budget (125 cyc).
- **borderline** — embeddable only after a specific simplification (noted).
- **watch-list** — expected to fail the cost gate today; track, never auto-promote.

## Recommendations — next codec to implement THIS cycle (embeddable, by expected gain/cost)

**CURRENT (cycle-16 survey, 2026-07-31).** Retired ledger re-checked: 20 registered codecs, **9
RETIRED** (`xchan_adaptive`, order-8 `xchan_bestpartner`, `iklt`, `iklt_adaptive`, `xchan_tans`,
`xchan_multiparent`, `xctx`, `LMS4rs+…bestpartner`, always-on `acar+bestpartner`). The leaderboard
best is `LMS4+Rice+xchan_bestpartner` (0.0394). Cycle 13 established that every **fixed** spatial
front-end now clusters within ~1% of a geometry-set ceiling, with the per-scale winner known
(single selected partner on tight arrays, jointly-solved best pair on large arrays), and that the
temporal-coefficient axis and the whole entropy back-end/context axis are spent **negative**.

The observation that opens this cycle: **all three spatial degrees of freedom the harness has spent
— which parent (selection), how many parents (count), which spatial scale (global/local) — are
defined at one fixed operating point that nobody has moved.** Every registered codec computes
`x[c] − (β·x[p])>>s` on **raw samples at the same time index**, then calls `lms_forward`. So the
spatial tap has always been (a) at **lag zero** and (b) in the **raw domain**. Both are, notably,
knobs the MPEG-4 ALS multichannel tool this project cites actually specifies — weighted subtraction
of the **residual** signals of two independently-predicted channels, with a selectable **time lag**
— i.e. the shipped `+xchan` is a lag-0, raw-domain simplification of its own reference recipe.
P1b's saturation result was measured over parent **count at lag 0** and says nothing about either
axis. This cycle's slate moves those two axes, plus the one live frontier lever:

1. **★ TOP PICK — Lag-aligned (propagation-compensated) best-partner spatial predictor
   (`LMS4+Rice+xchan_lagbp`, table J).** NOVEL; opens a genuinely new degree of freedom (time
   alignment of the spatial tap) that no cycle has touched. Per channel, per block i>0, scan over
   the previous **reconstructed** raw block BOTH the ≤4 causal grid neighbours AND an integer lag
   d ∈ {−D…+D} (D≈4), scoring estimated Rice bits of `x_c[n] − (β(p,d)·x_p[n−d])>>s` (reuse
   `_bp_opt_beta`/`_bp_score`); keep the min-bits (p,d*). Predict the block with ONE **joint**
   co-adaptive 2-tap sign-sign LMS on `{x_p[n], x_p[n−d*]}` — the **same** parent at two lags — so
   the instantaneous (volume-conduction) and propagating (delayed) components come out together
   without marginal double-counting. `d*=0` degenerates exactly to the shipped best-partner, so the
   construction is a **strict superset** of the promoted best and the min-bits gate bounds the
   downside. Zero side-info (the decoder mirrors the search from bit-identical reconstructed
   history). Why the physics says so: the array's pairwise cross-covariance is a **function of lag**,
   `R_cp(τ)`, peaking at the MUAP propagation delay `τ* = IED/CV`. At 10 mm inter-electrode distance
   and 3–5 m/s fibre conduction velocity that is **2–3.3 ms ≈ 4–7 samples at 2048 Hz** — about a
   quarter period (~90° phase) of the 60–120 Hz dominant sEMG band. For a neighbour **along the
   fibre direction** `R(0)` is therefore near zero while `R(τ*)` is large, so best-partner selection
   has been implicitly confined to **transverse**, non-propagating pairs and the entire propagating
   slice of cross-channel MI — `I(x_c[n]; x_p[n−τ*]) ≫ I(x_c[n]; x_p[n])` — has never been reachable
   by any codec here. This is the same delay the HD-sEMG conduction-velocity literature routinely
   estimates as a cross-correlation lag between upstream/downstream electrodes: a measured physical
   property of the signal class, not a codec hypothesis. **Causality: negative lags are legal at zero
   extra latency** — parent p has grid index < c and is fully decoded for the whole block before
   channel c is touched, so `x_p[n+d]` is already reconstructed; look-ahead stays exactly the existing
   block (clamp at block edges with the retained previous-block tail). Distinct from every relative
   on the axis it names decisive: **vs PROMOTED `bestpartner`** (lag 0, single tap → selected lag,
   joint 2-tap on the same parent); **vs KEPT `jointbp2`** (its second tap is a second **channel** at
   lag 0 — the DOF P1b proved empty on tight arrays; here the second tap is the **same** channel at a
   different **lag**, an axis never measured); **vs RETIRED `xchan_multiparent`** (summed *marginal*
   betas double-count the parents' shared mode; the joint co-adaptive gradient is the marginal→multiple
   fix, already proven in `joint2`); **vs RETIRED `iklt_adaptive`** (energy-preserving rotation
   corrupts both channels; this is asymmetric rank-1 residual-only injection, parents left clean).
   Verdict **embeddable at 2 kS/s sEMG, borderline at 30 kS/s neural**: state is D extra reconstructed
   int16/ch (~8 B; the parent block is already resident) and +1 multiplierless sign-LMS tap per
   sample-ch, but the per-block (2D+1)×|cands| search amortizes to ~36 ops/sample-ch at D=4 — fine
   against the 1875-cyc sEMG budget, tight against 125 cyc. Principled mitigation: `τ*` is a
   physiological constant within a recording, so re-search every k-th block (or cap D≤2) and the search
   cost falls by k at no ratio loss. Grounded in MPEG-4 ALS inter-channel prediction (selectable time
   lag + short cross-prediction filter) and HD-sEMG conduction-velocity delay estimation. Risk: if
   best-partner already lands on transverse neighbours whose `R(0)` is near the array maximum, the
   delayed tap may add estimation variance rather than MI on some arrays.
2. **NOVEL, ~cost-neutral — Residual-domain cross-channel prediction (`LMS4+Rice+rxchan_bp`, table K).**
   The second unmoved axis: the **domain** of the spatial operator. Stop decorrelating the raw array
   before the predictor; instead run the order-4 sign-LMS on each **unmodified raw** channel to get
   `e_c`, `e_p`, and code `e_c − (β·e_p)>>s`, choosing the partner and β by estimated Rice bits of that
   **actually-coded** quantity, per block and backward-adaptively (zero side-info, à la
   `bestpartner_adaptive`). Why (information-theoretic): coded length ≈ Σ log₂ σ_e, so the rate
   objective is the **residual's** entropy — yet the shipped front-end derives β by integer
   least-squares on **raw** signals and selects the partner by the Rice bits of the raw decorrelated
   channel (`_bp_score` is applied *before* `lms_forward`). Raw energy is dominated by the strong
   low-frequency shared power the temporal predictor removes anyway, so the shipped estimator buys
   correlation in a band the coder never pays for — a systematically mis-weighted proxy for the bits
   actually spent. Moving both the subtraction and its estimator into the residual domain makes the
   objective and the rate identical. This is **not** an algebraic rearrangement: each channel carries
   its own *adaptive* sign-LMS filter, and `P_c(x_c − βx_p) ≠ P_c(x_c) − βP_p(x_p)` whenever
   `P_c ≠ P_p`. Two secondary benefits fall out — each channel's LMS adapts on **clean raw data**
   instead of a partner-contaminated mixture (the same robustness argument P3-refinement credits the
   asymmetric subtract with), and on the decoder `e_p` **is literally the decoded symbol stream** of
   channel p, so obtaining it is free. Precedent is the harness's own top-cited sources: the MPEG-4 ALS
   multichannel tool performs "adaptively weighted subtraction of the residual signals of the coding
   channel from those of the reference channel, both produced by independent linear prediction", and
   Choi et al. (Sensors 2014) specify a normalized **residual** cross-correlation gate with an
   AbsMean-**residual** reference pick — i.e. the recipe cited for best-partner is a *residual-domain*
   coder, and the raw-domain simplification shipped here has never been tested against it. Distinct
   from every dead end: **NOT the RETIRED `xctx`** (that conditioned the Rice **parameter** on a
   cross-channel context, leaving both the residual and the front-end untouched; this changes what is
   subtracted from the residual itself), **not** an entropy back-end swap (the coder stays adaptive
   Rice), **not** a rotation (still an asymmetric rank-1 subtract), **not** a summed multi-parent
   (single selected parent). Verdict **embeddable, comfortably inside both budgets, ~cost-neutral to
   the promoted best (≈0.039)**: identical per-sample arithmetic (one int16 multiply-shift + one add)
   and it *removes* the separate decorrelated raw array. Cheapest possible attempt at the residual
   entropy floor P2/P5 name as the remaining bottleneck; if it wins it **composes** with #1 and with
   the selection/count/scale levers, since it moves a different axis. Risk: if neighbouring channels'
   LMS filters converge to near-identical taps the two domains nearly commute and the gain is small —
   but the head-to-head is at equal cost, so the test is clean either way.
3. **Scale-GATED spatial front-end: best-partner (C≤64) vs jointbp2 (C≥128)
   (`LMS4+Rice+xchan_scalesel`, table L).** INSIGHTS open-frontier #1, verbatim — the lowest

### Prior cycles' recommendations (kept for the record)

The cycle-10 slate spent all three prior frontier levers on real data and **none beat the
leaderboard best** `LMS4+Rice+xchan_bestpartner` (0.039): the two-stage cascade (`acar+bestpartner`)
helped only on tight OTB, the joint 2-parent solve (`joint2`) only TIED (parent selection and count
are **substitutes**, P1b), and the port-caveat closure (`bestpartner_adaptive`) succeeded as an
embeddability lever. The single-parent rank-1 subtract on order-4 remains the proven ratio ceiling —
three distinct attempts (wider basis, more parents, cheaper estimation) all landed at ≤ it. The dead
ledger holds 7 codecs: every entropy-back-end swap (P5), every context-model of the Rice **parameter**
(P5-ext), every multi-tap inter-channel rotation fixed OR adaptive (P3), the **summed** multi-parent
subtract (P1-ref), and the single-parent scalar β. This cycle's slate takes the three live levers off
INSIGHTS' refreshed open frontier — one per axis, distinct in mechanism, not parameter variants:
**stack** the two substitute spatial DOF (selection × count), attack the **temporal residual entropy**
the spatial saturation exposes, and **scale-select** the two spatial stages per recording.

1. **★ TOP PICK — Joint-solved SELECTED best pair: selection × count fused
   (`LMS4+Rice+xchan_jointbp2`, table G).** The highest payoff/cost live lever and the one
   combination cycle 10 did NOT try. Per channel, backward-adaptively SELECT the best PAIR of
   causal neighbours (per-block re-selection from the previous reconstructed block, decoder
   mirrors → zero side-info, exactly as the shipped `bestpartner_adaptive`), then predict c
   from that pair with ONE **joint** co-adaptive sign-sign LMS whose two spatial taps adapt
   against the SHARED post-subtraction residual (exactly as the kept `joint2`). Why the data
   says so (P1b): `joint2` delivered the highest Hyser cross-channel gain of any codec (+12.26%)
   via a second parent but LOST tight OTB because it used a FIXED up+left pair where SELECTION
   matters; best-partner won tight OTB via selection but is single-parent. Selection and count
   are **substitutes** used alone — this is the ONLY untried way to STACK them into a combined
   win: jointly solve the SELECTED best pair, so tight arrays get their dominant diagonal
   neighbour AND large arrays get the second jointly-solved parent's diffuse local MI. Distinct
   from every codec: **vs RETIRED `xchan_multiparent`** (summed marginal betas double-count the
   parents' shared mode; a joint gradient co-adapts and cannot — the marginal→multiple fix);
   **vs RETIRED `iklt_adaptive`** (energy-preserving rotation corrupts both channels; the joint
   predictor is asymmetric, rank-1 residual-only injection, parents clean); **vs KEPT `joint2`**
   (fixed pair → per-channel SELECTED pair); **vs PROMOTED `bestpartner`** (single selected
   parent → jointly-solved selected pair). Verdict **borderline → embeddable** (+1 spatial tap +
   per-block pair re-selection on order-4; gate on the neural 125-cyc budget; zero side-info).
   Distinct axis (spatial topology, selection × count) from #2/#3. INSIGHTS open-frontier #1.
   Risk: on tight arrays the second parent may still add little (joint2 lost OTB) — measure
   whether selection+count clears the best on BOTH array scales at once.
2. **NOVEL — Regime-switched (context-gated) order-4 temporal predictor
   (`LMS4rs+Rice+xchan_bestpartner`, table H).** The genuinely different axis: with every spatial
   lever within ~1% of a shared ceiling, the remaining bits live in the **temporal residual's own
   entropy** (P5 closed the coder and the Rice-parameter context; the residual entropy is set
   upstream by the *predictor*). Keep the proven order-4 best-partner spatial front-end unchanged,
   but replace the SINGLE temporal sign-LMS with a small BANK of order-4 sign-LMS predictors, one
   selected per sample by a backward-derived activity REGIME (a quantized bucket of recent
   reconstructed residual energy — quiescent vs MUAP-burst). Each regime's predictor adapts ONLY
   on its own samples, so its taps match that regime's local AR statistics instead of a single
   filter converging to a quiescent/burst compromise. Decoder mirrors the regime from causal
   reconstructed history → zero side-info; order stays ≤4 per P2. Why (information-theoretic):
   HD-EMG is strongly non-stationary and bursty; a single adaptive LMS UNDER-fits the
   high-variance bursts where most residual bits live. Regime-switched prediction lowers the
   CONDITIONAL residual variance H(e | regime) < H(e), lowering residual entropy UPSTREAM.
   **NOT the retired `xctx`** (that conditioned the Rice PARAMETER k on a cross-channel context,
   leaving the residual unchanged, and lost because H(e|neighbour energy)≈H(e) after LMS); this
   conditions the PREDICTOR COEFFICIENTS on a TEMPORAL activity regime, reducing the residual
   itself. **NOT** the retired entropy back-end swap (the coder stays adaptive Rice). Grounded in
   adaptive switching / context-dependent linear prediction (Seemann & Tischer; Ulacha
   MAE-minimizing context-switched prediction, Entropy 2024). Verdict **borderline → embeddable**
   (N×order-4 state + energy accumulator + regime compare; keep N small, 2–4). Distinct axis
   (temporal residual entropy) from #1/#3. INSIGHTS open-frontier #2. Risk: if order-4 already
   whitens the residual enough that per-regime AR statistics barely differ, the bank adds state
   for little gain — a cheap, clean test settles it.
3. **Scale-SELECTED two-stage spatial cascade (`acar?+lms4bp`, table I).** Salvage of cycle-10
   frontier #1, a distinct axis (spatial-scale meta-gating) but lower payoff. The always-on
   `acar+bestpartner` cascade helped only on tight 64-ch OTB (+0.81%) and was neutral/negative on
   the large 128/320-ch arrays (P1-refinement: the two MI slices are additive only where the
   global common-mode is a real eigenvector). Instead CHOOSE per recording between CAR-first vs
   best-partner-only, from a decoder-derivable global-vs-local coherence statistic (array channel
   count, or the ratio of array-mean energy to total residual energy over the first reconstructed
   block). Keeps the OTB max-ratio corner without the large-array regression, at zero side-info
   (the gate is a deterministic function of reconstructed data). Both stages are already shipped
   and verified — this is a per-recording meta-gate, not a new primitive → lowest mechanism risk,
   low-to-medium payoff (tight arrays only). Distinct axis (scale meta-gating) from #1/#2. INSIGHTS
   open-frontier #3, de-prioritized below #1/#2.
